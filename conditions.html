Concerns we commonly address

Persistent sadness, excessive worry, sleep difficulties or changes in daily functioning can all be reasons to seek professional support. None of these on its own means something is wrong — an assessment is what tells you more.

If there is immediate danger, do not wait for an appointment. Call 112 for emergency services or 108 for an ambulance. For free mental health support at any hour, Tele-MANAS — the Government of India helpline — is on 14416 or 1-800-891-4416.
