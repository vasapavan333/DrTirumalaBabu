Services

Psychiatric care for your mental well-being

Assessment, treatment and follow-up for common mental health concerns. Which of these applies to you is decided during the consultation, not beforehand.

Services offered at the clinic

Each of these begins with the same assessment. What follows is decided from it — there is no fixed package, and not every concern needs every element.

Psychiatric Consultation

A structured assessment of your symptoms, history and daily functioning, and a discussion of what the findings suggest.

More about this service

Counselling and Support

Supportive conversations and guidance where clinically appropriate, including CBT-informed, family and couple counselling.

More about this service

Stress and Anxiety Management

Professional evaluation and individualised care for persistent stress, worry, panic and phobia-related concerns.

Read about anxiety

Depression Care

Assessment and treatment planning for low mood, loss of interest, fatigue and related depressive symptoms.

Read about depression

OCD Care

Psychiatric assessment and evidence-informed treatment for intrusive thoughts and repetitive behaviours.

Read about OCD

Bipolar Disorder Care

Evaluation and ongoing management of mood swings and episodic changes in energy and behaviour, when clinically indicated.

Read about bipolar disorder

Schizophrenia Care

Assessment and treatment for psychotic symptoms and related concerns, with follow-up and family guidance.

Read about schizophrenia

Sleep Disorder Care

Evaluation of sleep-related concerns and the mental health symptoms that often accompany them.

Read about sleep problems

De-addiction Support

Assessment and treatment support for alcohol, tobacco and other substance use disorders, with follow-up care.

More about this service

Anger Management

Professional guidance for difficulty managing anger and related emotional difficulties.

Read about anger

Availability of a particular service on a given day depends on the clinical assessment and the clinic's schedule. Please call ahead if you are travelling for a specific concern.

How it works

What a consultation looks like

Four straightforward steps, so you know what to expect before you walk in.

  1. Book an appointment

    Call or message the clinic to request a consultation, or send an enquiry through the form below.

  2. Initial assessment

    Discuss your concerns, symptoms and relevant medical history. Family members are welcome if you would like them present.

  3. Treatment planning

    Explore the suitable options with the doctor — counselling, medication, or both — and why each is being considered.

  4. Follow-up care

    Review progress at agreed intervals and adjust the plan when clinically appropriate.
