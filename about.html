About the doctor

Meet Dr. Tirumala Babu

Dr. Manchikalapudi Tirumala Babu is a Consultant Psychiatrist with an M.B.B.S. and an M.D. in Psychiatry. He provides psychiatric care for individuals experiencing concerns related to mood, anxiety, behaviour, sleep and other mental health conditions.

Consultations at Maruthi Hospital and Diagnostics are built around a careful assessment: what you have been experiencing, how long it has been going on, and how it is affecting daily life. Where treatment is indicated, the options are discussed with you — and with family members where you would like them involved — before anything is started. Outcomes vary from person to person, and no result is promised.
